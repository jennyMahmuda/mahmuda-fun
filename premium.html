Premium Guidelines

Premium is being prepared for a future release. The current static site has no active checkout, subscription, payment collection or locked story API.

Planned premium experience

Future premium features may include ad-light reading, early series episodes, downloadable media where licensed, and member-only collections. Features, prices, refund terms and availability will be published before any payment is enabled.

User safety and content standards

Premium content will remain adult-only, fictional and consensual in presentation. Content involving minors, exploitation, non-consensual sexual activity or illegal material will not be accepted. Users must not share private personal information in reviews.

Before launch checklist

A payment provider, age-gate, account system, refund policy, data retention policy, customer support channel and server-side access control must be implemented before charging users. Do not send payment details through social messages or static-site forms.
