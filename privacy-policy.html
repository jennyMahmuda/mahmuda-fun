Privacy Policy

Working draft — review for the jurisdictions where the site operates. This policy describes the current static deployment of Nights (mahmuda.fun).

What we do not currently collect

We do not currently operate user accounts, sign-up forms, a first-party profile system, a first-party personal-data database, or a rating/review database. The site does not ask visitors to submit names, email addresses, phone numbers or payment details.

Analytics

Google Analytics is used only after a visitor selects “Accept analytics” in the cookie notice. It helps us understand aggregate traffic and performance. If analytics is rejected, the Google Analytics script is not loaded. Google may process data under its own terms and policies.

Local browser storage

The theme preference and cookie-consent choice may be stored locally in your browser. These values are not sent to a Nights server.

Advertising and external links

Third-party ad networks, social bars, native ads and sponsored links may set or read cookies or similar technologies under their own privacy policies. External websites, including linked social profiles, are independent services.

Future ratings and reviews

A future Cloudflare-backed rating and review system may collect a story identifier, rating, review text, moderation status, timestamps and abuse-prevention metadata. It is not active in this release. The system will be documented and consent/legal notices will be updated before activation.

Age restriction

Content is intended for adults aged 18 or older. Do not use the site if adult content is unlawful for you to access.

Contact and changes

We may update this policy when analytics, advertising, premium or review functionality changes. See the Cookies Policy, Terms and FAQ.
